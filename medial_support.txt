YouTube Fetcher

A fast, lightweight YouTube video downloader for Windows. Paste a URL, pick quality, and download — no ads, no bloat.

YouTube Fetcher is a clean, simple YouTube downloader built for Windows. It supports video and audio downloads in multiple quality formats, batch downloading, and keeps a history of your downloads. The app auto-updates from GitHub, so you always have the latest version. Built with Python and pywebview for a tiny 55MB footprint — no JavaScript frameworks, no Electron bloat.

Download YouTube videos and audio in one click. YouTube Fetcher is a minimal, fast desktop downloader for Windows that supports multiple quality options, batch downloads, and automatic updates. Just paste a YouTube URL, choose your format, and hit download. No ads, no popups, no nonsense. Lightweight at just 55MB with a clean dark interface.
